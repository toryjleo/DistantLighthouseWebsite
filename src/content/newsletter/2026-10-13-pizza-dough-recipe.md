<ul>
  <li>Flour</li>
  <li>350 g of water (95 degrees Fahrenheit)</li>
  <li>500 g of bread flower</li>
  <li>Pinch of yeast</li>
  <li>12 g salt</li>
  <li>2 tbsp olive oil</li>
</ul>





<div style="white-space: pre-wrap;">
Mix salt with water
Add yeast. Let hydrate (2 mins)
Stir
Add dough
Mix dough thoroughly using "pincer method"
== Pincer method gif ==
Wait 30 mins
Knead dough. Use lots of extra flour.
Cover dough in olive oil. Put in covered pot. Let sit 3 hours.
Separate dough into 3 balls. Use plenty of extra flour.
== Ball rolling gif ==
Put balls in covered container in fridge for 3 days. Pull out and let sit at room temperature 2 hours before use.
</div>
